Est. 2018 Chapter 01

Sourced from Origins Unknown.

We believe the finest tea leaves hold the precise geography, climate, and soul of the mountain soil they were plucked from.

"Our path is defined by direct-trade relationships, walking the high-elevation gardens of Taiwan, Japan, and Yunnan alongside generational master growers."

The Ethical Standard

By bypassing traditional multi-tier distribution hubs, we guarantee our growers receive premium compensation that far exceeds fair-trade baselines. This direct financial return protects their legacy, preserves ancient harvesting wisdom, and funds local ecological preservation programs.

Every seasonal micro-batch is traced back to an individual garden, providing transparent details about soil makeup, harvest weather, and oxidation craft.

Artisanal Preservation

From hand-rolled oolongs in Nantou to spring-picked gyokuro in Uji, we work exclusively with small family holdings who refuse high-yield industrial farming. Their careful, slower methods yield leaves with exceptional chemical complexity and deep mineral clarity.

We honor their craft in our physical atelier, storing each selection in climate-monitored vaults and sharing their preparation guidelines exactly as they were taught to us.
