N° 02

The Philosophy of Slow Steeping

Crafting balance between ancient agricultural heritage and modern daily life.

Our practice is built on a direct understanding of place. Every farm we partner with is dedicated to minimal intervention, clean soil chemistry, and the patient labor of hand harvesting.

Pure Terroir

We travel to high-elevation microclimates where cool morning mists slow the leaf development, concentrating natural sugars and amino acids for a deeper, more lingering finish.

Uncompromised Sourcing

We focus on rare cultivars and traditional processing styles, bringing you teas that tell the true story of the seasons and the hands that rolled them.
